Today, we are excited to announce the general availability of Large Message Support in Azure Event Hubs, enabling you to send and receive messages up to 20 MB in self-serve scalable Dedicated clusters, with enhanced reliability for seamless handling of large messages and greater flexibility for your data streaming solutions.
