Today we are excited to announce the general availability of confidential computing for Azure Service Bus Premium. With this capability, your Service Bus namespace processes messages inside hardware-based trusted execution environments (TEEs), preventing unauthorized access to data while it is being processed. This rounds out our protection story alongside existing encryption at rest and in transit, giving customers with regulatory or sensitive workloads a way to protect their messaging data through every stage of its lifecycle.
