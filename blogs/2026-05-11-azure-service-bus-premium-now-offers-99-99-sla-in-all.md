Today we are excited to announce an update to the Azure Service Bus Service Level Agreement . Starting May 1, 2026, all Premium namespaces deployed in regions with Availability Zone support will receive a 99.99% uptime SLA. This applies regardless of whether partitioning is enabled on the namespace.
